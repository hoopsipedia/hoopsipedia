# Unified Historical Rankings — Methodology (Prototype v0.1)

**Status: research prototype for Josh's review. Nothing here ships to the site.**

Generated by `unified_rankings.js` → `unified_rankings.json`. Deterministic: same
inputs always produce a byte-identical output file (no timestamps, no randomness).

This is the first cut at the #1 offseason project: ONE composite historical
ranking that aggregates every signal the repo already computes, instead of
maintaining HTSS, the efficiency engine, hardware counts, and poll history as
separate, occasionally contradictory leaderboards.

---

## Inputs

| File | What we take from it | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| `htss_v2_results.json` | `byTeam` — HTSS v2 score for every scored team-season (25,656 seasons, 348 teams) | All seasons HTSS could score |
| `efficiency_ratings.json` | `seasons` — adjEM (and within-season rank) per team per season | **1949-50 onward only** (77 seasons) |
| `seasons.json` | per-season W/L, SRS, AP poll marks, `ncaaTourney` result strings, coach, seed | Varies by program; SRS/AP gaps in early years |
| `data.json` (`H` table) | championships + title years, Final Fours, Elite Eights, Sweet 16s, tournament bids, all-time W/L, AP weeks ranked | All 365 programs |
| `net_rankings.json` | loaded as a current-era cross-reference only — **not weighted** in either composite (it covers one season; mixing it in would double-count the efficiency signal for 2025-26 only) | Current season |

Clone/duplicate team records are excluded using the exact same two-pass
detection as `htss_v2.js` (season-fingerprint matching + conference-mismatch
heuristic). 18 clones excluded; 347 programs ranked.

## Artifact 1: `programAllTime` (every program)

Each component is computed as a raw value, transformed where noted, z-scored
across the 347-program population, combined with the weights below, then mapped
to the familiar HTSS display scale (`50 + 15 × composite-z`).

| Component | Weight | Raw definition | Why this weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hardware** | 0.30 | `10 × Σ era-weighted titles + 4 × FF + 2 × E8 + 1 × S16 + 0.4 × bids`, then `log1p` before z-scoring | Banners are what programs are remembered for; it's the one signal fans will not forgive us for underweighting. `log1p` keeps UCLA's 11 titles from single-handedly deciding the whole list. |
| **HTSS program score** | 0.30 | Average HTSS of the program's top 10 seasons (same `top10Avg` as `computeProgramRankings` in `htss_v2.js`, read from `byTeam`) | HTSS is already our deepest 9-component per-season composite — this is the "sustained peak" signal. Equal to hardware on purpose: results vs. quality. |
| **Efficiency profile** | 0.15 | Mean of within-season adjEM percentiles across every season the program was rated | Rewards decade-over-decade statistical quality independent of tournament luck. Capped at 0.15 because it only exists from 1949-50. |
| **Win pct (longevity-adjusted)** | 0.15 | `(W + 100) / (W + L + 200)` — Bayesian shrink toward .500 with a 200-game prior | Raw all-time win pct lets a 30-year program with a hot stretch outrank a 120-year program. The prior makes longevity itself part of the signal. |
| **Poll prestige** | 0.10 | `log1p(AP weeks ranked)` | Public perception across 75+ years. Log because weeks-ranked is heavily right-skewed. Note: 0 weeks is treated as *real data* (never ranked), not missing. |

### Era weighting of championships
Only title **years** exist in the data (`H[id][7]`), so era adjustment applies
to championships only:

| Title year | Weight | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ 1949 | 0.70 | 8-team field, pre-integration talent pool |
| 1950–1974 | 0.85 | 16–25 team field |
| 1975–1984 | 0.95 | 32–53 team field |
| ≥ 1985 | 1.00 | 64+ team era — hardest path |

Final Fours / Elite Eights / Sweet 16s are bare counts with no years attached,
so they get flat weights. This slightly flatters old-money programs (UCLA's
pre-1975 Final Fours count the same as UConn's modern ones) — see Known Biases.

## Artifact 2: `seasonAllTime` (top 250 team-seasons)

Population: every team-season in `htss_v2_results.json` `byTeam` (~25.6K).
Components are on a shared z-scale, combined, and mapped to `50 + 15z`.

| Component | Weight | Raw definition | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HTSS v2** | 0.40 | `(htss − 50) / 15` — inverts HTSS's own display transform back to z-scale | Already a 9-component, era-normalized composite; it's the anchor. |
| **Efficiency z (era)** | 0.25 | z-score of adjEM against **all team-seasons in the same era** (6 eras, same boundaries as `htss_v2.js`) | Pure statistical dominance vs. era peers. Within-era (not within-season) so that a great team in a weak season doesn't get a free boost. |
| **Tournament result** | 0.20 | Same point ladder as `parseTournamentResult` in `htss_v2.js` (champion 15 → missed −2), rescaled so champion ≈ +3.0z | What the season is remembered for. Yes, HTSS already includes a tournament term — double-weighting March is a deliberate editorial choice for an "all-time seasons" list. |
| **SRS (era)** | 0.15 | z-score of SRS within era | The only major signal *not* derived from our own engine — an independent cross-check that damps any efficiency-engine quirks. |

Missing-season rows in `seasons.json` (no record/tournament/SRS) and pre-1950
seasons (no adjEM) trigger weight redistribution, not zeros.

---

## Missing-component rule (both artifacts)

Mirrors `htss_v2.js` exactly: when a component is `null` (data does not exist),
its weight is redistributed **proportionally** across the components that do
have data:

```
adjustedWeight_i = weight_i / Σ(weights of available components)
composite        = Σ available value_i × adjustedWeight_i
```

So 1950s CCNY isn't punished for the efficiency engine starting in 1949-50 or
for missing AP coverage — it is judged on what we can actually measure.
Components are never silently imputed to average.

Pre-Modern (≤1964) · Integration Era (1965–85) · Early Modern (1986–93) ·
Mid Modern (1994–2007) · Late Modern (2008–15) · Current (2016–).

## Known biases

1. **Pre-1949 data is largely absent.** No efficiency ratings before 1949-50,
   spotty SRS/AP earlier. Redistribution means early-era programs are ranked
   mostly on hardware + win pct — coarser instruments. Helms-era "titles"
   aren't in `H` at all, so 1900s–1930s powers are structurally invisible.
2. **Pre-AP perception gap** (same caveat as the HTSS notes): the AP poll
   begins in 1948-49, so poll prestige is zero-by-construction for everything
   before it, and z-scoring `log1p(apWeeks)` across all programs mildly
   penalizes long-dead programs whose entire run predates the poll. They're
   counted as "never ranked" rather than "missing" — debatable, flagged for Josh.
3. **Final Four / E8 / S16 counts are not era-weighted** (no years in the data),
   so deep runs in 8- and 16-team fields count the same as modern ones. Benefits
   UCLA/Kentucky/UNC; penalizes UConn/Villanova-style modern dynasties.
4. **HTSS appears twice in spirit.** The season composite weights HTSS 0.40 and
   then adds tournament and efficiency terms that HTSS already contains. This
   intentionally sharpens the all-time list toward champions, but it means the
   season list is *more* March-flavored than HTSS alone.
5. **Tournament field-size bias in seasonAllTime**: a 1956 champion needed 4-5
   wins; a 2024 champion needs 6. The tournament ladder doesn't discount for
   this (HTSS's own era normalization partially compensates).
6. **Conference labels are current-day** (`data.json H`), e.g. UCLA shows
   "Big Ten" — cosmetic only, not used in scoring.

## Open tuning questions for Josh

1. **Hardware vs. sustained quality (0.30/0.30 split).** Right now banners and
   HTSS program score are equal partners. Should hardware dominate (a "what did
   you win" list) or should HTSS (a "how good were you" list)? Moving hardware
   to 0.40 pushes UConn and Villanova up and Illinois/Purdue-type
   "great-but-ringless" programs down.
2. **Should `seasonAllTime` double-count March?** With tournament at 0.20 on
   top of HTSS's internal 0.18 tournament term, all 15 of the current top 15
   are champions. If you want 1990-91 UNLV / 2014-15 undefeated-regular-season
   Kentucky-style teams to surface, drop tournament to ~0.10 or zero it and let
   HTSS's internal term carry it.
3. **Pre-AP and pre-1950 programs: redistribute or zero?** AP weeks are
   currently *zero* (real data) for pre-poll programs but efficiency is *null*
   (redistributed). Consistent alternative: treat AP as null for programs whose
   last season predates 1949. Also: do you want a hard "minimum 20 D-I seasons"
   filter, or is shrinkage (the 200-game prior) enough?

## Reproducing

```
node unified_rankings.js
```

Reads the four JSON inputs from repo root, writes `unified_rankings.json`
atomically (tmp + rename). All weights live in the single `WEIGHTS` object at
the top of `unified_rankings.js`.
